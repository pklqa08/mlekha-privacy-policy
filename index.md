# mLekha Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

mLekha is a personal-finance application that helps users track income, expenses, and financial transactions on their Android device.

mLekha does not require users to create an account or sign in. Transaction and financial information is intended to be processed and stored locally on the user's device, rather than uploaded to a server operated by the developer.

## 2. Information the app accesses and processes

Depending on the features you enable and the permissions you grant, mLekha may access or process:

- **SMS messages:** Relevant financial messages, such as bank transaction alerts, to identify potential income, expenses, and transfers.
- **Payment notifications:** Notifications from payment or banking apps that you choose to enable for transaction detection.
- **Financial transaction information:** Amounts, dates and times, transaction descriptions, transaction types, merchant or counterparty names, and account details included in a financial message.
- **Information you enter:** Transactions, categories, notes, budgets, and other financial information you enter into the app.
- **App preferences:** Settings such as sync periods, enabled sources, and other preferences needed for the app to operate.

The app uses these data only for its personal-finance and transaction-management features.

## 3. How information is used

Information processed by mLekha is used to:

- Identify and organise eligible financial transactions.
- Help you track income, expenses, and transfers.
- Categorise and display transactions and summaries.
- Avoid importing the same transaction more than once.
- Apply your selected settings and preferences.

SMS and notification access are used for transaction-related functionality, subject to the permissions and settings you choose.

## 4. Storage and sharing

Transaction records and financial information are intended to remain on your device. The developer does not operate a server to which mLekha uploads your transaction records or SMS contents.

The developer does not sell your personal or financial information.

The app's Android operating system, app distribution services, or any third-party libraries included in the app may process limited technical information according to their own practices. The developer must verify those components' actual behaviour before claiming that no information of any kind ever leaves the device.

## 5. Permissions and your choices

mLekha may request SMS permissions and notification access for transaction detection. These permissions are used for the features described in this policy.

You can manage permissions in Android Settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the corresponding transaction-detection feature from working, but you can continue to use features that do not depend on that permission.

Grant only the permissions you are comfortable with.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Information stored by mLekha remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to the app's actual storage and Android backup behaviour.

You can delete individual transactions using the app's available controls. Deleting a transaction may also create local records used to prevent an intentionally deleted transaction from being imported again.

If you want to remove all locally stored app data, use Android's app-data controls or uninstall the app. This may permanently remove your transaction history and settings. Verify any backup or export you need before doing so.

## 7. Security

mLekha is designed to process financial information locally, reducing the need to transmit that information to a developer-operated server.

You should protect your device with a secure screen lock, keep Android updated, and avoid sharing access to your device. No method of electronic storage or software can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 8. Children's privacy

mLekha is intended as a personal-finance tool for its declared target audience. It is not specifically designed to collect children's personal information. The app's target audience and handling of information must comply with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

This policy may be updated when app functionality, permissions, data handling, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at the privacy-policy URL provided in the app's Google Play listing.
